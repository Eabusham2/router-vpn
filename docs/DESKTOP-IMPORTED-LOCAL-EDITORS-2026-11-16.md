The Windows, Linux and macOS ordered editors now preserve each independently
imported Shadowsocks hop at its original interior position in supported Local
paths of three through eight members. Paired WireGuard/AmneziaWG members retain
their original positions and the final remains a paired Shadowsocks exit. Each
external member must expose the exact Boolean
`ordered_local_imported_nodes_supported` capability from its captured catalog.

The editors preserve the full node/mode ordering and source-token inventory
through capture, Save/Load and Connect. They send private-node references, not
copied passwords or bundled protocol assets. Relinked members, duplicate IDs,
missing or non-Boolean capabilities, substituted protocols, an imported entry
or final, and an incompatible final are rejected. This Local addition does not
borrow Server/Compare authority or widen the existing SOCKS5, CONNECT or OpenVPN
position contracts. The desktop controller and ordinary latency readers were
published as separate prerequisite units.
