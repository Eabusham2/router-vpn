# Android measured candidate rows and complete profile proposals — 2026-11-16

## Scope

This change keeps a completed TCP candidate measurement visible when a known complete-profile layout cannot be saved. Each row carries either an individually compiled complete proposal or a precise Save-unavailable reason. A valid proposal in another row remains available. The measurement remains TCP handshake timing through the captured prefix; it does not establish authentication, complete-path connectivity, or installed-device acceptance.

The complete proposal retains every original member outside the measured replacement position, its execution choice, and its final policy. The only row-level exception is a private checked exception constructed after a fresh original-result check for an explicit layout limitation: a retained duplicate identity, an imported replacement outside the penultimate position, another retained imported intermediate, an unsupported retained final transport for that import, or a measured nonpacket transport replacing a packet intermediate. Source mutation, expired or foreign results, cancellation, network or owner changes, and unexpected native/compiler failures still invalidate the batch.

## Ownership and UI

The preparation checks its original source and result before and after every proposal operation, including the narrow incompatibility catch. It publishes results only after the original temporary service, candidate worker, captured handles, and MTU hold have joined. Completed result authority remains bound to the captured source, network, Home epoch, settings, and original result object.

The Save picker contains only current, individually compiled proposals and indexes its filtered list. Incompatible rows remain visible with their measured value and explicit reason. If every row is incompatible, pressing Save retains those rows and reasons. Saving remains separate from explicit Load and Connect.
