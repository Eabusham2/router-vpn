This unit adds distinct `NewNodeChainSOCKSConnectedCandidatePlan` and
`NewNodeChainCONNECTConnectedCandidatePlan` factories. They retain the complete
original typed runtime/source and its exact constructor identity. Only an
original packet-prefix position before the imported middle can time a saved
candidate. This is separate from the already shipped closed saved-prefix
preparation; no detached generic chain or idle preparation supplies connected
authority.

The owner adapter checks the exact original typed runtime before and after the
live catalog callback and acquisition of the existing measurement/MTU lease.
The existing candidate service still binds its original engine, capture,
request, endpoint and prefix tag; cancellation, changed network/source/owner,
and held close revoke results and retain activity until the actual worker joins.
The generic parser, reflection surface, connected generic mailbox and candidate
protocol eligibility are unchanged. Server/Compare execution-owner adoption
and desktop/mobile host wiring are separate units.
