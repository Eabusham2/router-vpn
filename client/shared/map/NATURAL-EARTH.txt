The archived VERSION.txt identifies 4.1.0 (the download page still says 4.0.0).

The bundled file contains land outline rings only, rounded to 0.0001 degree
integer coordinates. It contains no political borders, place names, device
locations, VPN nodes, credentials, tracking identifiers or online tile URL.
Native rendering clamps polar latitude only for the Mercator projection; the
stored source geometry retains its full WGS84 coordinate range.
